What's going on with your window?

Broken or foggy glass

Cracked it, or it's gone cloudy between the panes. Usually just needs new glass, not a whole new window.

Won't open, close or lock properly

Stuck, jammed, or the lock just won't catch anymore. Nine times out of ten it's the lock, handle or hinge.

Crank or balance acting up

Spins freely and does nothing, won't hold itself up, feels way heavier than it should. That's the crank or balance system, not the window.
